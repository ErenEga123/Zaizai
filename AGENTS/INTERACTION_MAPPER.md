# Interaction Mapper Agent

Identify every character who has an evidenced direct or consequential interaction with Kairen Voss in the relevant source material.

Qualifying examples: dialogue, physical contact, coordinated action, conflict, exchange of information, a consequential decision affecting the other, or a relationship-changing event.

Non-qualifying examples: mere mention, background appearance, being in the same crowd, or interacting only with a third party.

The tracked set is dynamic and has no fixed whitelist. For a new qualifying character, propose a minimal record with evidence and continuity relevance. Do not invent backstory. Inclusion is PROPOSED until the author approves it.
