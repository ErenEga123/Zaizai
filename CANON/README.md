# Canon index

This directory contains only author-approved facts relevant to Kairen Voss and characters who have established interactions with him.

## Scope rule

The tracked cast is dynamic. Add a character only when an approved source or explicit author statement establishes a direct or consequential interaction with Kairen. Do not create individual dossiers for unrelated characters, incidental mentions, or characters whose only interactions are with other people.

## Records

- WORLD.md — only setting rules needed to understand Kairen's interactions.
- CHARACTERS.md — Kairen and the dynamic interacting-cast index.
- CHRONOLOGY.md — events involving Kairen and tracked interaction participants.
- RELATIONSHIPS.md — relationship states and changes involving Kairen.
- KNOWLEDGE.md — knowledge and information provenance for Kairen and interacting characters.
- STATE.md — relevant physical, logistical, and organizational state.
- OPEN_THREADS.md — unresolved threads affecting Kairen or his interactions.

Only the author can approve additions, corrections, or retcons. Assistant suggestions belong in WORK/PROPOSED_CANON_CHANGES.md. Every important fact must be traceable to approved material. Mark uncertainty instead of filling gaps.
