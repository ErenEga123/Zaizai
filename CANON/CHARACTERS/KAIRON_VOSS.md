# Kairon Voss

**Record status:** CANON — author-confirmed character facts  
**Narrative role:** Original secondary character added to the existing story  
**Tracking role:** Focus character for this repository's interaction-continuity scope; this does not make him the original story's protagonist.

## Identity

- **Name:** Kairon Voss
- **Species:** Human
- **Homeworld:** UNKNOWN. Born somewhere on a remote, poorly specified planet in the Outer Rim.
- **Affiliation:** Republic military, after graduating from a military academy.
- **Academy cohort:** Joins the existing group of four academy graduates as its fifth member.
- **Romantic partner:** Sumeragi Li Noriega. They began dating while attending the military academy.

## Background

As a child, Kairon was abducted by an unknown group and subjected to experiments. The experiments involved artificially inducing or enhancing sensitivity to the Force and physically augmenting his body. The exact organization, facility, methods, dates, and duration are UNKNOWN.

Kairon eventually escaped. The circumstances and precise age at the time of escape are UNKNOWN.

After his escape, a retired Republic officer found him, took him in, and raised him as his own son. The adoptive father’s earlier service and reason for retirement are intentionally outside the scope of this dossier. When Kairon decided to apply to the military academy, his adoptive father trained him for admission, including military discipline and combat preparation.

## Force sensitivity

Kairon has heightened sensitivity to the Force, but he is **not** a Jedi and cannot perform conventional telekinetic or defensive Force techniques.

Established capabilities:
- Senses the Force and unusual disturbances through it.
- Experiences danger premonitions and can sometimes anticipate the direction or onset of an attack.
- May sense a threat or presence without being able to identify exactly what it is.

Established limitations:
- Cannot pull objects toward himself or push them away using the Force.
- Cannot create Force shields.
- Cannot use other conventional Jedi-style active Force techniques.
- His premonitions are not reliable, complete visions of the future and do not guarantee that his body can avoid an attack.

The exact range, reliability, triggers, and precision of his sensing remain UNKNOWN.

## Physical augmentation and overdrive

The experiments physically augmented Kairon. His body is substantially beyond an unmodified human baseline, particularly in combat-relevant physical performance. His enhanced capabilities include strength, speed, reaction, coordination, and endurance.

Kairon can temporarily force his body to release a greater portion of its available potential. This is a short-lived emergency overdrive, not a Force technique.

**Cost:** Overdrive causes severe physiological backlash. After using it, Kairon can suffer intense exhaustion and physical impairment. The precise symptoms, safe duration, recovery time, and long-term medical consequences remain UNKNOWN.

His enhancements do not make him invulnerable. Exact upper limits and vulnerabilities have not yet been established.

## Character and temperament

- Generally calm and reserved.
- Prefers to avoid unnecessary conflict and resolve problems without aggression.
- With people close to him, he becomes more affectionate, open, and expressive.
- He is especially tender and openly affectionate with Sumeragi.
- He is disciplined and normally follows lawful military orders and the chain of command.
- He may disobey an order when it concerns something deeply important to him or conflicts with his personal boundaries and principles. He accepts that such a choice may have consequences.

## Combat profile

Kairon is intended by the author to be an exceptionally capable, near-ideal combatant. His principal strengths are:
- Hand-to-hand combat.
- Firearms and shooting.
- Combat tactics and battlefield assessment.

His augmented body, heightened reactions, and Force-based danger sense reinforce one another. Force sensitivity can warn him of danger or help him anticipate an attack; his enhanced nervous system and body help him respond.

This does not grant omniscience, guaranteed victory, or immunity to surprise, simultaneous attacks, superior positioning, or other battlefield constraints. Exact skill rankings, preferred weapons, tactical specialties, and service assignment remain UNKNOWN.

## Relationship with Sumeragi Li Noriega

Kairon and Sumeragi are both graduates of the same military academy cohort. They began a romantic relationship during their academy years. Kairon is her boyfriend.

**Knowledge boundary:** Sumeragi does not know about Kairon’s childhood abduction, the experiments, his artificially enhanced Force sensitivity, or his physical augmentation. Kairon deliberately keeps this past from her because he does not want to burden her with it. This is concealment motivated by a wish to spare her distress, not by a lack of trust.

Do not change Sumeragi’s established personality, history, professional competence, or decisions merely to accommodate Kairon. Her characterization must remain grounded in the original text.
