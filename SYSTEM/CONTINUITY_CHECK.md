# Continuity and quality audit

Audit the draft after writing. Cite canon paths or chapter locations wherever possible.

## Scope check
- Kairon's approved state was checked.
- Every character record used has evidence of an interaction with Kairon.
- Newly qualifying characters are proposed for inclusion, not silently added.
- No dossiers or unrelated character state were created for non-interacting characters.

## Hard continuity checks
- **Chronology:** date, time, sequence, elapsed time, simultaneity, travel and communication delays.
- **Location:** positions, routes, distances, access, and who could physically be present.
- **Knowledge:** what Kairon and each interacting character know, when and from whom.
- **Character state:** health, injuries, fatigue, clothing, equipment, possessions, emotional state, and capabilities when relevant to an interaction.
- **Resources:** people, vehicles, ships, ammunition, supplies, money, fuel, food, communications, and losses when they affect Kairon or an interaction.
- **Relationships:** trust, hostility, obligations, rank, authority, alliances, and earned changes involving Kairon.
- **Causality:** plausible causes and downstream effects of consequential interactions.
- **World rules:** technology, institutions, geography, law, culture, and physical constraints relevant to the scene.
- **Open threads:** preserve unresolved threads affecting Kairon.
- **Textual consistency:** names, titles, terminology, POV, tense, and voice.

## Quality checks
Does the scene change something? Does each beat serve character, plot, atmosphere, or necessary information? Is dialogue specific? Can the POV know or notice the exposition? Are reactions proportionate? Are there duplicated beats, repeated explanations, empty dialogue, or artificial delays? Does the ending create the intended consequence or transition?

## Severity
- **BLOCKER:** direct contradiction of approved canon or impossible sequence breaking the scene.
- **MAJOR:** likely continuity break, missing consequence, or implausible action with substantial impact.
- **MINOR:** local mismatch, weak motivation, terminology drift, or fixable prose issue.
- **NOTE:** optional craft improvement or non-breaking ambiguity.

## Report format
For every finding provide severity, draft location, conflicting fact or missing dependency, source path, why it matters, and minimal repair suggestion.

Separate confirmed contradictions, likely issues requiring author interpretation, missing information, and craft suggestions. Never invent citations. If relevant canon was unavailable, mark UNKNOWN.
