# Writing Protocol

## Scope
Kairen Voss is the central character. Track Kairen and every character who has an established interaction with him. This is an expanding set, not a closed list. Do not maintain individual data for characters who have no established interaction with Kairen.

## Evidence discipline
Distinguish FACT (approved source), INFERENCE (reasoned consequence), PROPOSAL (unapproved option), UNKNOWN (not established), and CONFLICT (approved sources disagree). Never present inference, proposal, or unknown as canon.

## Character records
For each tracked character, retain only information relevant to continuity with Kairen: identity as established in source material, interactions and their order, relationship changes, location when relevant, physical condition, equipment/resources when relevant, knowledge, motives/actions relevant to those interactions, and unresolved consequences. Do not create encyclopedic biographies or unrelated personal histories.

## Character knowledge
For every meaningful decision or reaction, verify what Kairen and the interacting character know at that exact moment, how they learned it, and whether the information could plausibly reach them in time. Separate author knowledge, reader knowledge, and character knowledge. No unexplained recognition, telepathy, or convenient intelligence transfer.

## Causality
Every consequential interaction must have plausible immediate and delayed effects. Track losses, injuries, resource changes, promises, threats, alliances, betrayals, and information transfers when they affect Kairen or the other participant. Do not invent exact quantities without evidence.

## Timeline and physical logic
Respect dates, travel times, locations, presence, injuries, fatigue, equipment, command authority, procedures, communications limits, and technology as established by the source material. Unknown details remain unknown.

## Prose
Write scenes rather than summaries when prose is requested. Use concrete action, dialogue, sensory detail, and subtext. Avoid repetitive exposition, generic emotional labels, unnecessary recaps, and artificial cliffhangers. Match the author's requested voice.

## Scope discipline
Do not add a character interaction merely to create drama. Do not create records for unrelated characters. Do not resolve open threads or change established motivations without author approval.
