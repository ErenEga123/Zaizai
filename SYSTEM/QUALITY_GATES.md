# Quality Gates

A chapter is not marked continuity-clean unless applicable checks were performed against relevant repository sources.

## Scope gate
- Kairon's approved state was checked.
- Every character dossier used has evidence of an interaction with Kairon.
- Newly qualifying characters are proposed for inclusion, not silently added.
- No unrelated character dossier or independent state record was created.

## Source gate
- Relevant approved sources were loaded.
- Factual findings cite exact repository paths.
- UNKNOWN and CONFLICT are explicit.
- Drafts and proposals are not treated as canon.

## Continuity gate
- Timeline, travel, location, presence, physical condition, equipment, resources, relationships, and consequences are consistent.
- Consequences of earlier interactions are represented.
- No unsupported numerical or logistical details were invented.

## Knowledge gate
- Kairon and each interacting character act only on information they could possess at that moment.
- Information has a plausible source and transmission path.
- Reader knowledge, POV knowledge, and character knowledge are distinct.

## Narrative gate
- Scene purpose and conflict are clear.
- Motivation follows established characterization and available information.
- Requested POV, tense, scope, and voice are respected.
- No filler, redundant recap, or unearned resolution.

## Severity
BLOCKER: direct canon contradiction, impossible knowledge, broken chronology, or causality failure that changes the scene.
MAJOR: missing consequence, implausible motivation, spatial/technical failure, or characterization drift.
MINOR: repetition, pacing, wording, or local clarity.
NOTE: optional craft suggestion.

A blocker must be fixed or explicitly referred to the author. Never conceal a failed gate.
