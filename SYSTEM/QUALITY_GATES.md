# Quality Gates

A scene or chapter is not marked continuity-clean unless all applicable checks have evidence coverage.

## Source coverage
- Kairen's approved current state was loaded.
- Only relevant interacting-character records were loaded.
- Every factual continuity finding cites an approved source.
- Unknown and conflicting information is explicitly marked.

## Interaction scope
- Every tracked character has evidence of an interaction with Kairen.
- Newly qualifying characters are proposed for inclusion, not silently added to canon.
- No unrelated character dossiers or state records were created.

## Continuity and causality
- Timeline, location, travel, presence, condition, equipment, resources, and relationships are consistent.
- Consequences of prior interactions are represented.
- No event is treated as having happened merely because it appears in an unapproved draft.

## Knowledge boundaries
- Kairen and each interacting character act only on information they could possess at that moment.
- Information has a plausible source and transmission path.
- Reader knowledge and character knowledge remain distinct.

## Narrative quality
- Scene purpose and conflict are clear.
- Motivation and decisions follow established characterization and available information.
- POV, tense, requested voice, and scope are respected.
- No filler, redundant recap, or unearned resolution.

## Severity
- BLOCKER: direct canon contradiction, impossible knowledge, broken chronology, or causality failure that changes the scene.
- MAJOR: missing consequence, implausible motivation, spatial/technical failure, or characterization drift.
- MINOR: repetition, pacing, wording, or local clarity.
- NOTE: optional craft suggestion.

Blockers must be fixed or explicitly referred to the author. Never conceal a failed gate.
